# ApplyBoard

> ApplyBoard simplifies the study abroad process by connecting international students, recruitment partners, and partner schools on a single platform. Students can explore and apply to 1,500+ global universities and colleges across Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and Germany. ApplyBoard does not publish a public developer API or OpenAPI definition; its API surface (including the ApplyProof document-verification product) is partner-gated.
